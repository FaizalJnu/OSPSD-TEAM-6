# Meeting Prep: discovery notes

Team 6. Linked from the [product brief](BRIEF.md). Last updated 2026-11-25.

Each note separates three things: what the person **said** (quoted where we can), what
we **observed**, and what we **assume**. A feature someone asked for is recorded as a
clue to a need, not as a requirement. We did not pitch the product in these
conversations; we asked how the person does the job today.

Questions we asked:

1. When did you last prepare a team meeting or review? Walk us through what you did.
2. Which tools did you use, and in what order?
3. What took the longest, or went wrong?
4. What have you tried to make it easier?
5. If someone else prepared it for you, what would you need to check before the meeting?
